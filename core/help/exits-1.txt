EXIT STATUS
    0   the thing was done. For `hw next` that means the task was DELIVERED and
        the counter advanced; for a launch, that the launch request completed.
        Vendor runtime readiness may remain unestablished; read the receipt.
    1   every refusal, without exception. hw has exactly one failure code, and
        every refusal it prints comes from one `die`. So a caller may branch on the
        status alone: a gate that would not open, a container with no agent
        left in it, an agent that did not start and an unreachable pane are all
        1, and none of them is ever reported as success.
    Two usage exits are not refusals: `hw` alone with no terminal to draw its
    picker on prints the usage and exits 2; `hw <one word>` prints it and exits 64.

    `hw wait` additionally reports VERDICTS, which are not refusals — the wait
    ran, and these say what it found about the TARGET. 1 still means hw refused
    (bad argument, unknown pane, missing cross-check library) and never that the
    executor is in some state. The numbers match state-witness.sh's own, so the
    toolchain has one number space:
    2   the budget was spent and the pane never reached idle/done. The ordinary
        timeout, and what an `unknown` cross-check costs: nothing extra.
    5   SETTLED — proved unable to change. A larger budget buys nothing.
    6   AWAITING-HUMAN — its own endpoint confirms an outstanding prompt.
    7   ENDED AND NEVER REPORTED — turn_state=ended_unreported together with
        done_state=undelivered. No wait is started: the report exists, it did
        not arrive, and the turn that would have retried it is over.
    8   HAND-BACK REFUSED — the executor ended a turn with no report, no
        delivered ask/challenge outstanding, and no named one-shot human boundary.
    3/4 herdr-rpc transport: could not connect / rpc error. Facts about herdr,
        not about the executor.
